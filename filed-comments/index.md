# filings/

The project's submissions on public dockets. Rule of the shelf: what is filed is
published here **verbatim, as filed**, with docket, date, and tracking number; drafts
are marked as drafts until then. Nothing summarized, nothing paraphrased — the
administrative record and this folder should be diffable.
