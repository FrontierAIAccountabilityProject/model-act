# what's in the act

*errata notice (17 aug 2026): five claims on this card describe the act as it is being amended to be, not as v3.3 reads today; one commencement line was wrong and is corrected in place below. every entry, with the exact statutory text: [ERRATA.md](../ERRATA.md).*

*section numbers below follow the campaign explainers for the current draft; defer to the act text in the repo root as authoritative, since numbering shifts between versions.* → [read the act](../model_act_v3_3.txt) · [the companion](../model_act_v3_3_companion.md)

## the highlights reel

- **the halt rule** — whoever had the power to stop the model answers for the model. delegating it to a committee does not save you. (SEC. 4)
- **the receipts rule** — the CEO personally signs a safety certification every quarter, like bank CEOs have done since 2002. no signature, no shipping. (SEC. 8)
- **the 72-hour rule** — your model escapes or gets stolen? you report it in 72 hours. and deleting the logs doesn't stop the clock, because the clock starts when your own monitoring *would* have caught it. we thought of that. (SEC. 9)
- **the girl math that isn't** — fines get clawed from the exec's own bonuses, and the company is **legally banned from paying it for him.** he cannot expense his consequences. (SEC. 7)

## the guest list: who's invited to consequences (and who isn't)

- **NOT liable: you, the engineer.** written into the definitions: rank-and-file employees are exempt. this law climbs UP the org chart only. your manager's manager's manager is sweating, not you.
- **very liable: the officers at the top.** the officers with actual authority to say "don't ship." maybe ten people on earth. that's the whole guest list.
- **and the whistleblower gets PAID.** report a violation, receive 10 to 30 percent of the penalties. NDAs that try to gag you? **void.** telling the truth becomes the best-paying job at the company. (SEC. 11)

## it was never going to be you

a handful of models on earth clear the line. your fine-tune, your weekend model, your use, study & modification of weights: untouched. sec. 1 says so, out loud. someone answers for the mechanism — not you, dear engineer.

## we invented almost nothing

california, new york & illinois already drew the same lines: models above 10²⁶ operations, ~$1m penalties, mandatory audits (illinois passed that 110–0). the only new part is **jail for the men in charge** — and that part is 80 years old. egg executives did real time under it. (SEC. 10 · *US v. Park*, 1975)

## what changed in v3.3

v3.2 → v3.3 in one day, in public, with receipts. now harder to switch off, and easier to adopt:

1. **it survives the weather.** washington is drafting bills to switch off state AI laws. so the act got a severability ladder: knock out one limb and the rest stands. if the federal law lapses, ours wakes back up. (SEC. 13 · SEC. 3(c))
2. **truth counts from day one** — lying to the state, hiding an incident, shredding the records: offenses from the effective date, no agency required. the core shipping offense arrives on a fixed clock — day [180], running on interim standards borrowed verbatim from enacted california, new york & illinois law — so an agency that never materializes can never switch the act off. (SEC. 3(c)) *(corrected 17 aug 2026; this line previously said "it starts working day one" — [ERRATA.md](../ERRATA.md), E6.)*
3. **it was never going to be you** — the exemptions above, stated out loud in sec. 1.

## the whole act, plainly (shitpost version)

- SEC.1 the stuff in SEC.3 = crimes. same legal lane that jailed the egg execs.
- SEC.2 applies to any giant model + whoever sells it. no hiding behind a subsidiary.
- SEC.3 CRIMES: shipping it uncontained · the model hacking things · not reporting · lying about it.
- SEC.4 the CEO is liable if he COULD have stopped it. didn't know? doesn't matter. egg CEO rules for you, CEO!
- SEC.5 CEO signs a safety cert every quarter, personally. lying on it = felony. "should have known" counts.
- SEC.6 report incidents in 72h. someone else finding it first doesn't pause your clock.
- SEC.7 jail up to 1yr, 3 for repeat/fraud. any state AG can sue + halt the model. lose govt contracts.
- SEC.8 whistleblowers get protection AND a cut of the fine. the inspectors already work at the labs.
- SEC.9 works in all fifty states. file everywhere.

## the pre-debunked excuses

- **"i didn't know"** — 1943 called. knowing was never required. that's the whole point of the doctrine.
- **"i delegated safety"** — the rat warehouse guy tried that. delegation does not divest.
- **"it'll kill innovation"** — pharma, banking, and aviation all carry this rule and all still exist. eggs survived accountability. the models will cope.
- **"you can't regulate what you don't understand"** — we don't regulate the math. **we regulate the man.**
