# For the review council — the reviewer's edition

*This page exists so that no reviewer ever needs the rest of the repository. Five seats,
one core set, one lane each. Everything not named on this page is context you are licensed
to skip: the `/dossier` is evidence assembled for journalists, `/docs` are lay explainers,
`/archive` and `/pages` are history. Your time goes to the text.*

**The standing terms, restated.** Scope in writing before you start; roughly 10–20 hours
across eight weeks, adjustable; unpaid; your disposition is published as written, including
"approved with reservations" and including hostile. Under the project's own published rule,
nobody — including the maintainers — may claim this text "survived review" until named
reviewers sign. That rule is why these seats exist. The current text is a research draft
and says so; treat every claim as checkable, because checkability is the only authority an
anonymous project has.

## The lanes

**Seat 1 — Criminal law** *(culpability, elements, defenses)*
The statute's SEC. 1 (classification), SEC. 4 (controlling persons), SEC. 5–6 (prohibited
acts; individual liability), SEC. 10(b)–(c) (penalties). In the cure queue: cures 2
(narrowed authority, express exclusions), 5 (proximate cause), 13 (privilege). In the
audit: [chunk3](./audit/chunk3_penalty_architecture.md) and
[chunk4](./audit/chunk4_harm_tier_rebuild.md). Core questions: do the elements hold as
charged offenses; is the due-care floor the right floor; do the defenses that are absent
belong absent.

**Seat 2 — Enforcement and prosecution** *(would anyone actually charge this, and what breaks first)*
SEC. 5 (the acts), SEC. 9 (reporting), SEC. 10 (enforcement), SEC. 12 (limitations,
records, successors). [chunk3](./audit/chunk3_penalty_architecture.md) for the enacted
family's numbers; [chunk5](./audit/chunk5_commencement_and_records.md) for commencement
and the records offense. Core questions: provability, charging practicality, what an AG's
office does with this in year one.

**Seat 3 — Frontier security** *(are the duties technically real)*
**[`model_regulations_v1_draft.md`](./model_regulations_v1_draft.md)** is your primary
text; then the statute's SEC. 2 (the duty and the modification-evaluation budget), SEC. 3
(standards and validation), SEC. 9(a) (incident classes, the near-miss rule). In the cure
queue: cures 11 (autonomous external access), 12 (certification cadence), 14
(contained-by-design). Core questions: where the text meets lab practice, and where
practice would laugh.

**Seat 4 — Open source and academia** *(the powerful reached, the commons untouched)*
SEC. 1(b)(9) (release and the weights carve-out), SEC. 1(b)(1) (lineage), SEC. 2 (the
budget). In the cure queue: cures 1 (the wrapper rule), 9 (lineage default and the audit
floor), 16 (controlled research). Core question: do the release provisions actually
deliver the promise — duties climbing to those with power to halt, freedoms flowing down
to everyone else — or do they leak.

**Seat 5 — Fiscal and administration** *(can a state honestly afford and run this)*
SEC. 10(a) and (f) (penalties, the fund), SEC. 11 (awards), SEC. 3 (what the Agency must
actually do). [chunk3](./audit/chunk3_penalty_architecture.md) §D for how the numbers were
set. Standing fiscal rule to hold us to: enforcement is never sold as self-funding,
penalties are never booked as revenue, startup is stated apart from steady state. Core
question: does the administrability story survive contact with a real budget office.

## Time budget

**First hour:** the statute, straight through. **First sitting (2–3 hrs):** add ERRATA and
your lane's cure entries. **Full pass (10–20 hrs):** your lane's companion notes and audit
chunks, then your disposition. Anything beyond that is your generosity, not the scope.

## Filing a disposition

Email **llmaolaw@proton.me** — links or pasted text, no attachments. Any form you like: a
memo, a marked-up copy, a numbered list of findings. It is published as written, credited
or anonymous at your choice (council seats publish with names; that is the point of them).
A finding that something is broken is the seat working, not failing: catches land in
[`ERRATA.md`](./ERRATA.md) with the fix attached, and the record of who caught what is
permanent.

## What you are not being asked to do

You are not asked to endorse, to co-author, to join a campaign, or to lend standing beyond
your written disposition. The campaign layer is deliberately separate and will never quote
you into it. You are asked to do the one thing the project cannot do for itself: be a
named expert who checked.
