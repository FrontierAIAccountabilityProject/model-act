# Revision

*How this Act is being repaired, and what is still wrong with it.*

## Start here

**[Worklist](./worklist.md) — every open item.** Sixty-nine of them: four defects in the operative
provisions, twenty-three drafted amendments, four decisions, twelve unanswered objections, fourteen
unconfirmed facts, and twenty citations that need a law library. **Every row says who can close it**
— the maintainer by reading a file, a lawyer, a librarian, or whoever can find a document that is
not held. Fifteen close by reading. Sixteen need a lawyer.

**[Proposals](./proposals.md) — the drafted repairs.** Replacement text for each amendment, with the
anchor it splices into. **None is adopted, and nine have not been validated by the maintainer.**

## Live, and being worked on

Those two. Everything below is a dated record of how the draft reached its current state, kept
because the drafting record is part of what this project publishes. **None of it is current, and
where any of it disagrees with the worklist, the worklist is right.**
